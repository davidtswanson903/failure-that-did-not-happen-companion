# The reserve's series

- **Source:** US Energy Information Administration: *U.S. Ending Stocks of Crude Oil in SPR*, annual, and *U.S. Net Imports of Crude Oil and Petroleum Products*, annual.
- **Addresses:** https://www.eia.gov/dnav/pet/hist/LeafHandler.ashx?n=PET&s=MCSSTUS1&f=A and https://www.eia.gov/dnav/pet/hist/LeafHandler.ashx?n=PET&s=MTTNTUS2&f=A
- **Retrieved:** Nov 2, 2026, the tables released Oct 30, 2026.
- **Licence:** public domain, as EIA's terms state it: https://www.eia.gov/about/copyrights_reuse.php
- **Fields:** the reserve's stock at the end of each year, in thousands of barrels, from 1977, and net imports, in thousands of barrels a day.
- **The obligation and the sales:** Congressional Research Service reports R45577 (2019), IN11916 (2022) and IN12542 (2025), https://www.everycrsreport.com/reports/R45577.html
