# The permit series

- **Source:** San Francisco Planning Department, *Planning Department Records - Projects*, DataSF dataset `qvu5-m3a2`.
- **Address:** https://data.sf.gov/resource/qvu5-m3a2.json
- **Retrieved:** Nov 2, 2026. The record is updated daily, and this is the series as it stood that day.
- **Licence:** public domain, as the dataset states it.
- **Fields kept:** `record_id`, `record_status`, `open_date`, `close_date`, `number_of_units_prop`, for every record with `number_of_units_prop` above zero. They are one department's records, and reviews other departments make are not in them.
